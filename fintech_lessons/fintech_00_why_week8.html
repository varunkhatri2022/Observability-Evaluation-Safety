What a senior engineer asks before shipping

A beginner asks "does it work?" A senior asks four more questions. Click each one: these are today's four modules.

Module A
When it gives a wrong answer, can I see WHY in two minutes?
ObservabilityTraces: every step, every prompt, every chunk the model saw.
Module B
How good is it, as a number? And did my last change make it better or worse?
EvaluationDatasets, evaluators, experiments. Measure, don't guess.
Module C
What is the worst thing it could say, and what stops it?
GuardrailsLeaked data, prompt injection, unsafe advice. Prompts ask; code enforces.
Module D
What does it cost at 10,000 questions a day, and where does the money go?
CostTokens, before/after, caching. Cheaper only if quality holds.

This never ends, and that is the job

Observe→ Evaluate→ Guard→ Measure cost→ Improve→ back to Observe

Models change. Users find new ways to break things. Costs grow with success. Teams that win are not the ones with the cleverest prompt; they are the ones who keep this loop running.
